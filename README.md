Running AI locally sounds simple, but there’s an important detail worth understanding: not every feature in an AI app necessarily runs locally.

A text rewrite might happen entirely on your Mac, while asking an AI to analyze an image or search the web could require a network connection.

When AI features may send data to a cloud provider

Why local search and indexing don’t automatically mean the final answer stays local

The trade-offs between local models and cloud-based AI

How to make a more informed decision about privacy

The goal is to look beyond the “runs locally” label and understand what actually happens behind the scenes.
